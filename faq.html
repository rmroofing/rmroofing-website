Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our roofing services, quotations and coverage areas.

Do you offer free quotations?

+

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotations for roofing work across Wokingham, Reading, Bracknell and the surrounding Berkshire area. Get in touch and we'll arrange a time that works for you.

What areas do you cover?

+

We cover Wokingham, Reading and Bracknell directly, along with Barkham, Woosehill, Earley, Winnersh, Crowthorne, Finchampstead, Woodley, Henley and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just ask.

How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

+

This depends on the age and condition of your roof and the extent of the damage. We'll give you honest, practical advice on the best option for your property, without pressure to choose a more expensive solution than you need.

How long does a new roof installation take?

+

This varies depending on the size and complexity of the roof, the materials used and the weather. We'll give you a clearer timeframe as part of your quotation once we understand the scope of the work.

Do you handle emergency roof repairs?

+

We understand that some roofing issues, like leaks, need prompt attention. Call us on 07766 985573 to discuss your situation and we'll advise on next steps.

What roofing materials do you work with?

+

We work with slate, tile, flat roofing systems and leadwork, among others. We can advise on the most suitable materials for your property during your quotation.

Do you carry out work on period or listed properties?

+

Yes, we carry out heritage roof restoration work, taking a sensitive approach to original materials and detail on period properties.

How do I get started?

+

Call us on 07766 985573, email ross@rmroofing.co.uk, or fill in the contact form on our Contact page and we'll get back to you to arrange a free quotation.

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk through your roofing needs directly.
