About Us

Reliable Roofing, Built on Experience

RM Roofing is a reputable roofing company in Berkshire with over 35 years of experience, known for reliability, professionalism and high-quality craftsmanship.

Our Story

A trusted name across Berkshire

At RM Roofing, we provide reliable, high-quality roofing services for homeowners across Wokingham, Reading, Bracknell and surrounding Berkshire areas. Whether it's a small repair, a full replacement roof, leadwork repairs or a new flat roof, we deliver practical roofing solutions built to last.

With over 35 years of experience, we've built our reputation on honest advice, dependable workmanship and roofs that stand up to British weather. From slipped tiles and leaking roofs to complete new roof installations, every job gets the same level of care, attention to detail and focus on long-term performance.
