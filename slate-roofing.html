Slate Roofing

Slate roofing is a durable, attractive option for many Berkshire homes. We carry out slate roof repairs, replacements and new installations with a focus on clean detail and lasting performance.

What's Involved

  • —Repair or replacement of individual damaged or slipped slates
  • —Full slate roof replacement where needed
  • —New slate installations on extensions or full re-roofs
  • —Careful handling to match existing slate where appropriate
